About us

Built around one question from a waiting room.

“So what do I eat tomorrow?” It sounds simple, but the answer rarely arrives in a usable form. Not a calorie table, not a list of bans, but a meal someone can actually cook at home.

Diabest Friend exists to close that gap: turning nutrition guidance into small daily decisions, what to buy at the market, how much goes on the plate, and what to write down before the next check-up.

What we hold to

Support your doctor, never replace them

The app helps you walk into the consulting room with tidy records; it does not stand in for the person examining you.

Local ingredients, fair prices

Menus are built from what the nearest market sells. Healthy should never mean expensive or foreign.

No scare tactics

Diabetes is heavy enough without added guilt. We write like a friend, not a supervisor.

Your data is yours

Everything you enter stays on your own device; see our Privacy Policy for details.

How we write our articles

Every article is drafted from established public health guidance (such as Indonesia's Isi Piringku plate guide and standard diabetes diagnostic ranges), not reviewed by a licensed health professional. The last-updated date sits under the headline. If you spot something wrong, write to us; corrections are published.
