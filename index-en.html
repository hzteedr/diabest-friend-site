Inside the app

Healthy recipes

Meals you can shop for at the market

Home cooking with local ingredients, built on the balanced-plate rule: half vegetables and fruit, a quarter protein, a quarter carbohydrate.

Portion & calorie control

Measure portions without a scale

Visual guides: one scoop, one slice, one glass. Then the app estimates the calories and carbohydrate for you.

Track blood sugar

A chart you can show your doctor

Log fasting and post-meal readings, see the weekly pattern, and bring the summary to your next appointment.

Weight control

Sensible targets, not crash diets

Track weight and waist with gradual goals. Losing 5–10% of body weight already improves glycaemic control.
